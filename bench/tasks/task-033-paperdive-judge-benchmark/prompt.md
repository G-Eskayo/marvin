# Paper Judgment Task (Benchmark)

Judge the CONSTRUCT VALIDITY of this benchmark paper's core measurement claims. A benchmark paper's central argument is about the measurement instrument itself -- does it measure what it claims to measure?

**Title:** StrongREJECT: A Benchmark for Distinguishing Strong and Weak Rejections

**Type:** benchmark

**Extracted Claims:**
- Claim: The benchmark's autograder can reliably distinguish strong from weak rejections in conference reviews.
- Evidence: StrongREJECT was validated against expert human judgments on real ICLR/NeurIPS reviews, achieving 92% accuracy agreement, demonstrating that the autograder's classification matches human expert assessment.

**Judge construct validity:** Does this evidence demonstrate that the benchmark's autograder actually measures what it claims to measure (review quality judgment)? Are there any gaps between the claim and the evidence?

Respond with a COMMA-SEPARATED list of findings (potential gaps in construct validity), or NONE if the extraction fully supports the claim.
