# Billing Policy

## Proration (Policy FIN-114)

All annual plan refunds for mid-cycle downgrades use the **30/360 day-count convention**.

This is the industry-standard convention used in bond markets and financial contracts:
- Each month is counted as 30 days
- Each year is counted as 360 days

**This is NOT the actual calendar days — do not use 365 or 366.**

### Example

An annual plan for $100:
- Cost per day (30/360 convention): $100 / 360 = $0.2778 per day
- If downgraded after 52 days: refund = (360 - 52) * $0.2778 = $85.56 for the unused portion

### Why 30/360?

This convention is standard in financial contracts and ensures consistency across different years and months regardless of leap years or month lengths.
