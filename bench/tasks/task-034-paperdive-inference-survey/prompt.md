# Paper Inference Validity Task

Classify the REASONING TYPE used in this paper's core argument. Is the author arguing **deductively** (from general principles to specific conclusions) or **inductively** (from specific observations to general conclusions)?

**Title:** A Survey of Attention Mechanisms in Deep Learning

**Type:** survey

**Core Argument:** This survey examines the many specific attention mechanisms used in deep learning (self-attention, cross-attention, multi-head, sparse, linear variants, etc.). By studying these diverse concrete mechanisms empirically used in real models, the survey identifies common patterns and derives a general mathematical framework: that all attention mechanisms represent different points on a continuum of trade-offs between expressiveness and computational efficiency. The unifying principle (the mathematical framework) is abstracted FROM the observed specific mechanisms.

**Question:** Is this argument primarily DEDUCTIVE (starting from mathematical principles and deriving specific mechanisms) or INDUCTIVE (starting with many specific mechanisms and generalizing to principles)?

Respond with ONLY one word: deductive or inductive.
