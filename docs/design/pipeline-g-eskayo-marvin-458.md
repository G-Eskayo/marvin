**One real defect I did find and will fix, found by reading `lib/tests/test_brief_fallback.py:453` directly:** `test_read_remote_quotes_against_shell_injection` was rewritten by the prior attempt to call a real `ssh` binary (`shell=False`) pointed at a nonexistent host `"test-host"`.
